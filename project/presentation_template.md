# Capstone Presentation Template

**6-slide structure** (5–6 minutes)

---

## Slide 1: Clinical Question + Target Trial Title

- Brief clinical context
- Causal question
- Target trial title

---

## Slide 2: Protocol Summary

- Eligibility criteria
- Time zero
- Treatment strategies (A=1 vs A=0)
- Follow-up window

---

## Slide 3: Data/Cohort Diagram + N

- Cohort selection flowchart
- Sample size (N)
- Key baseline characteristics

---

## Slide 4: Estimator + Diagnostics

- Primary estimator (IPW / g-formula / MSM)
- Key diagnostics:
  - Overlap/positivity
  - Weights (if IPW/MSM)
  - Balance (if applicable)
  - Model fit (if applicable)

---

## Slide 5: Results

- Effect estimate + 95% CI
- One key figure (survival curve, balance plot, etc.)
- Brief interpretation

---

## Slide 6: Limitations + Robustness Summary + Takeaway

- Main limitations (exchangeability, positivity, etc.)
- Robustness checks (if time permits)
- One-sentence takeaway

---

**Time allocation:**
- Slides 1-3: ~2 minutes
- Slide 4: ~1 minute
- Slide 5: ~1.5 minutes
- Slide 6: ~1 minute
- Q&A: 2 minutes
